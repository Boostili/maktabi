# P2-06 — Design Preview Modifier

Status: complete
Blocked by: P2-01

## Objective

Make Maktabi Agent Preview distinguishable from Production at Windows taskbar and installer sizes without modifying the core symbol.

## Deliverables

- Small-size comparison of at least three external modifier treatments.
- Approved `branding/maktabi/source/maktabi-agent-mark-preview.svg`.
- Production-versus-Preview proof at 16, 24, 32, 48, and 256 px.

## Completion evidence

Approved 2026-09-27. Three external treatments were compared; the four-corner bracket was selected because it remains visible at 16 px without changing the core symbol or implying an error state.

## Rules

- Obsidian/White only.
- Modifier sits outside or around the core mark.
- Do not use an error badge, notification dot, gradient, or text as the only small-size distinction.

## Acceptance

- Production and Preview are distinguishable at 16 px.
- Preview still reads unmistakably as Maktabi Agent.
- Modifier does not reduce the books below their production minimum weight.
